1. Introduction

ZEN Z TECHNOLOGIES respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how the KADAI MITRA application handles information when you use our shop management application.

2. Information Stored

Users may enter the following information:

  • User name and mobile number
  • Shop name and shop details
  • Login password
  • Sales and expense records
  • Customer names, mobile numbers and credit records
  • Supplier details, purchase records and payment information

3. Data Storage

The current version stores application records locally on the user's device using local storage. It does not use a cloud server to store or synchronize these business records.

The current version stores login passwords locally without encryption. Users should protect their devices and avoid sharing access with unauthorized people.

4. Information Sharing

We do not sell users' personal information. The current version does not include third-party analytics or advertising SDKs, and business records are not transmitted to our servers.

If users choose to copy and share payment reminder messages through WhatsApp or another application, the privacy practices of that external application will apply.

5. Customer and Supplier Information

Users are responsible for ensuring they have appropriate permission to enter and manage information about their customers and suppliers.

6. Data Security

Application records are stored in the app's private device storage. However, the current version does not encrypt stored application records. No electronic storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Records remain on the device until deleted by the user or removed when application data is cleared or the app is uninstalled. Please keep a backup of important business records before uninstalling the app or clearing its data.

8. Children's Privacy

KADAI MITRA is designed for business owners and is not intended for children under 13 years of age.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when application features or data practices change. Updated versions will be published on our website.
